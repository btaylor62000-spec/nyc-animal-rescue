# More situations, answered by Animal Help Now

_Every question Animal Help Now answers, from bats and glue traps to a cat up a utility pole, linked to its answer and to the New York City guide._

Animal Help Now (ahnow.org) is a national service that finds the nearest wildlife help for any US address. Its resources page answers about seventy questions, from a baby bird on the ground to a cat stuck on a utility pole. Every one of them is listed here, linked straight to its answer, with this site's New York City guide beside it where there is one. Animal Help Now writes for the whole country, so where it says "animal control" or "your local shelter", the New York City contacts are in the guide named. Read 2026-11-05.

## WILDLIFE EMERGENCY

- I found a baby bird: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#babybird. In New York City: [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird).
- I found a baby duck, goose or other ground-nesting bird: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#babygroundbird. In New York City: [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird).
- I found a baby owl, hawk or eagle: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#babyowl. In New York City: [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird), under hawks and owls.
- I found a baby mammal: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#babymammal. In New York City: [Found injured or orphaned wildlife](/guides/found-wildlife).
- I found a baby squirrel: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#babysquirrel. In New York City: [Found injured or orphaned wildlife](/guides/found-wildlife).
- I found a turtle: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#turtle. In New York City: [Found injured or orphaned wildlife](/guides/found-wildlife), under wild turtles.
- I found a reptile: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#reptile. A pet reptile loose in the city: [Lost or found a rabbit, bird or reptile](/guides/lost-and-found-exotic).
- I found a bat: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#findbat. In New York City no rehabber may take bats: see raccoons, skunks and bats in [Found injured or orphaned wildlife](/guides/found-wildlife).
- Is it really orphaned, or are the parents just out of sight? https://ahnow.org/resources.php#orphan
- Should I try to reunite a baby animal with its parents, and how? https://ahnow.org/resources.php#reunite
- My cat or dog caught a bird, rabbit or mouse: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#catdogattack. It is an emergency even with no wound: see "If it was caught or bitten by an animal" in [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird).
- A bird hit my window: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#birdhitwindow. In New York City: [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird). Every bird that hits a window needs a rehabber.
- An animal is stuck in a glue trap: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#gluetrap
- An injured deer: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#deerneedshelp. In New York City: [Found injured or orphaned wildlife](/guides/found-wildlife).
- An animal seems to have mange: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#animalmange
- Should I try to rehabilitate a wild animal myself? https://ahnow.org/resources.php#rehab. In New York it is illegal without a licence.
- A wild animal is used to people (habituated): https://ahnow.org/resources.php#habituate
- How do I capture and transport a wild animal? https://ahnow.org/resources.php#transportwild. For birds in New York City: [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird).
- Can I get help transporting wildlife? https://ahnow.org/resources.php#transporthelp. In New York City, for birds: the Urban Wildlife Alliance helpline, in [Found an injured bird](/guides/found-an-injured-bird).
- I am not in the United States: https://ahnow.org/resources.php#nonUSA
