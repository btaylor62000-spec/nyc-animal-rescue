# Cats needing specialist help

_Neonatal kittens, critical medical cases, special needs, seniors and FeLV+/FIV+ cats._

Use this when a specific cat needs specialized help. Always confirm current capacity - specialists fill up fast, especially in kitten season.

## NEONATAL / BOTTLE-BABY (unweaned kittens needing round-the-clock care)

- Itty Bitty City Kitties (Queens/Rockaway) - dedicated neonatal nursery; the specialist. ittybittycitykitties@gmail.com | ittybittycitykitties.org
- Bay Ridge Kittens (Brooklyn) - solo neonatal/critical-kitten rescuer (Flatbush Cats med coord). Instagram @bayridgekittens (contact via IG/adoption form)
- Kittens and Barbells Rescue (Bronx) - TNR-cert + neonatal tube/bottle-feed trained. kittensandbarbellsnyc@gmail.com | dolphin-coyote-9c2r.squarespace.com | Instagram @kittensandbarbells_
- Cat Castle NYC (Manhattan) - takes kittens as young as one day old. (973) 752-7750 | catcastlenyc.org | Instagram @catcastlenyc
- Noah's Ark Animal Rescue NY (Queens/Rockaway) - noahsarkny.com (contact form). Catstoria/Kitty Haven (Queens) - catstoriarescue@gmail.com | catstoria.com

## SICK / INJURED / CRITICAL-MEDICAL (cats needing emergency or intensive vet care)

- NY Cat Warriors (multi-borough) - urgent/complex medical; approved ACC rescue partner. nycatwarriors.org | Instagram @nycatwarriors
- NYC Second Chance Rescue (Queens) - critically injured/neglected from ACC 'death row'. nycsecondchancerescue.org | Instagram @nycscr
- Little Wanderers NYC (Bronx) - neediest cats, heavy vetting. email@lwnyc.org | lwnyc.org
- Puppy Kitty NYC (multi-borough) - severe medical/cruelty cases. info@puppykittynyc.org | puppykittynyc.org
- Catstoria / Kitty Haven (Queens) - sick & injured ferals + recovery house. catstoriarescue@gmail.com | catstoria.com
- Strays Hope for Life (Staten Island) - 'sick, bruised, or broken.' (908) 986-2505 | strayshopeforlife@gmail.com
- Sean Casey Animal Rescue (Brooklyn) - takes in sick and injured animals; ring first to check it has room. (718) 436-5163 | info@nyanimalrescue.org

## SPECIAL-NEEDS / DISABLED (3-legged, neurological, blind, chronic conditions)

- K9Kastle (South Brooklyn Petco catteries) - 3-legged/neuro/wobbly; the specialist. k9kastle@gmail.com | k9kastle.org
- Tavi & Friends (Brooklyn-based) - special-care + lifetime foster. 646-872-1533 | adopt@taviandfriends.org | taviandfriends.org
- Saint Spiro (Queens) - special-needs/injured 'ones nobody wants.' (347) 622-2630 | saintspiro007@gmail.com
- For Blake's Sake (Staten Island) - special-needs incl. blind cats. (732) 413-5006 | forblakessakerescue@gmail.com
- Cat Castle NYC (Manhattan) - hard cases others won't take. (973) 752-7750 | catcastlenyc.org

## SENIOR CATS

- Tavi & Friends (Brooklyn-based) - senior + FIV lifetime care; the specialist. 646-872-1533 | adopt@taviandfriends.org
- Cat Castle NYC (Manhattan) - through-senior (up to 16 years). (973) 752-7750 | catcastlenyc.org
- KittyKind (Manhattan) - senior + special-needs placement. adopt@kittykind.org | kittykind.org
- Kittens and Barbells (Bronx) - all ages incl. seniors. kittensandbarbellsnyc@gmail.com | dolphin-coyote-9c2r.squarespace.com | Instagram @kittensandbarbells_

## FeLV+ / FIV+ (retrovirus-positive cats)

- Tavi & Friends (Brooklyn-based) - FIV lifetime care. 646-872-1533 | adopt@taviandfriends.org
- Bronx Tails (Bronx/Harlem) - FeLV+ adoption specialty. bronxtailscatrescue@gmail.com | bronxtails.org
- Empty Cages Collective (Brooklyn/citywide) - FeLV+ and special-needs. 1-800-880-2684 | adopt@emptycagescollective.org
- Victory 4 the Voiceless (Staten Island) - FIV+/special-needs vetted. victory4thevoiceless@gmail.com
- For Animals Inc. (citywide) - takes retrovirus-positive cats. foranimalsinc.com | Instagram @foranimalsinc

## WORKING-CAT / BARN PLACEMENT (un-adoptable ferals placed as working cats)

- Urban Cat League (citywide) - working-cat placement + taming education. urbancatleague.org/contact-us
- For Animals Inc. (citywide) - working-cat options. foranimalsinc.com
- Hard Hat Cats (citywide) - places ferals at businesses. hardhatcats@gmail.com | hardhatcats.org
- ACC Working Cats Program (municipal) - workingcats@nycacc.org | (212) 788-4000 | nycacc.org/workingcats

## FURTHER AWAY: SPECIALISTS OUTSIDE THE CITY (when nobody here has room)

- The Odd Cat Sanctuary (Massachusetts) - medical, behavioral, feral, senior and "death row" cats, mostly in foster homes. theoddcatsanctuary@gmail.com | https://linktr.ee/theoddcatsanctuary (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Tabby's Place (Ringoes, New Jersey) - cage-free sanctuary for cats from hopeless situations, with its own vet care. (908) 237-5300 | info@tabbysplace.org | https://www.tabbysplace.org (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Spicy Cats Rescue (Exeter, New Hampshire) - medical and behavioral special-needs cats, newborns to hospice. meow@spicycats.org | https://www.spicycats.org; strays and surrenders by its intake questionnaire (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- King's Pride Kitten Rescue (central New Jersey) - orphaned kittens 0-8 weeks, sometimes to 12; by email first, arranged in advance; no owner surrenders. adoptions@kings-pride.org | https://kings-pride.org (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Veterinary Technicians for Cleft Affected Animals (California, by email) - education and support for kittens and puppies born with a cleft palate. techsforclefts@gmail.com | https://www.techsforclefts.com (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Only Maine Coons Rescue (Absecon, New Jersey) - Maine coon cats. info@omcrescue.org | https://www.omcrescue.org (read on its own site by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Pets Alive (Middletown, New York, about 90 minutes from the city) - a no-kill shelter whose animals are often older, have medical needs or need specialized care; dogs, cats and farm animals. (845) 386-9738 (dog team ext. 2, cat team ext. 3), info@petsalive.org | https://petsalive.org (read by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Kizzy's Place (South Jersey and Philadelphia) - foster-based cat rescue, "the Island of Misfit Cats". 856-238-1229 | info@kizzysplace.org | https://kizzysplace.org (read by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).
- Fosterlings (Pottstown, Pennsylvania) - kitten rescue. (610) 329-4266 | fosterlingsinc@gmail.com | http://fosterlings.org (read by a reviewer, 2026-11-05).

Confirm current capacity before relying - specialist intake is the first thing that closes when a group is full.
