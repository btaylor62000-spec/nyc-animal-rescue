# Low-cost clinics and trap banks

_How to get a cat spayed or neutered for free or cheaply, where to borrow a humane trap, and dental care for feral cats._

Every low-cost clinic and trap bank in the directory is listed at the end of this page. This page explains how to use them. Hours and eligibility change, so confirm before you book.

## BEFORE YOU BOOK

- Most free spay/neuter for community (street) cats is only for Certified TNR Caretakers. How to become one is in the guide on community cats: [Community cats, colonies and starting TNR](/guides/starting-tnr)
- A cat that cannot be handled must go to a clinic experienced with feral cats, in a covered trap, one cat to a trap. Ask the clinic before you trap.
- For a pet cat, the ASPCA's free clinics are for owners on public assistance who live in particular zip codes; each clinic's listing says which. The Humane Society of New York does low-cost spay/neuter for any cat or dog.

## CITYWIDE (work in every borough)

- The Toby Project - FREE community-cat spay/neuter, all 5 boroughs (Certified TNR Caretakers). Brooklyn, Manhattan and Staten Island: (347) 816-2899. Bronx/Queens mobile: Tobyprojectnyc@gmail.com.
- ASPCA - free/low-cost spay/neuter citywide: public via Same-Day Waitlist (clinic.aspca.org); rescue groups through the ASPCA's Rescuer Services Scheduler (https://www.aspca.org/veterinary-services/rescue-professionals). Physical clinics in South Bronx + LIC Queens; mobile vans in designated zips.
- Humane Society of NY (HSNY) - 306 E 59th St, Manhattan - (212) 752-4840 - low-cost spay/neuter for all cats (pet + feral) + dogs, 7 days, appointment only. Each cat in a separate covered trap.
- Friends of Animals - certificates, redeem at feral-friendly vets - 1-800-321-PETS.

## TRAP BANKS / HUMANE TRAP LENDING (borrow a trap to start TNR)

- Neighborhood Cats Trap Bank - reserve at neighborhoodcats.org/tnr-in-nyc/trap-banks. Pickup: Public Storage, 30 Prince St, Brooklyn 11201.
- ASPCA Trap Bank - for rescuers who already have an ASPCA rescuer account (it is not taking on new New York rescuers as of 2026-11-04); email TNRtrapbank@aspca.org.
- NYC Feral Cat Initiative (Bideawee) - trap loans for Certified TNR Caretakers via the helpdesk (nycfci.happyfox.com/new).
- Good Home Pet Adoptions (Crown Heights) - community TRAP BANK, info@goodhomepetadoptions.org.

## DENTALS FOR FERAL / UNHANDLEABLE CATS (bigger than a spay/neuter - needs a feral-experienced clinic, done under sedation in the trap)

- Park Slope Veterinary Center - 639 4th Ave, Brooklyn 11232 - (718) 369-7387 - BEST lead: feral-friendly + Dr. Szacki's practice explicitly does dental work (cleanings/extractions) and works with TNR caretakers.
- BBAWC Rescue Clinic - 76 Montague St 11201 - (917) 426-3335 - feral-friendly, does sedated exams/basic care; ask if the dental is in scope or a referral.
- Faithful Friends (2455 McDonald Ave, Brooklyn - (718) 339-7387) + Animal General (558 Columbus Ave, Manhattan - (212) 501-9600) - feral-friendly list; call to confirm they'll do a dental on an unhandleable cat.
- Long Island Animal Surgery (Patchogue/Glen Cove) - advertises low-cost dental cleanings/surgeries for FERAL cats, no extra handling charge - worth the trip for a bigger dental.
- HOW TO ASK: 'This is a feral/unhandleable cat needing a dental - can you sedate in the trap and do the dental assessment + extractions under that same anesthesia?' Confirm scope, bloodwork, and cost BEFORE trapping. A dental is bigger than a spay/neuter, so use a full-service feral-friendly clinic, not a high-volume spay/neuter clinic.
