# Breed-specific dog rescue

_Rescues that focus on a particular breed or group of breeds._

Use when an adopter or surrenderer wants (or has) a specific breed/type. Most are breed-club networks that cover NYC through a regional/NYC-area volunteer coordinator rather than a local shelter, so ALWAYS confirm current NYC-area capacity and intake. All are ACC New Hope partners unless noted.

"BULLY" / MOLOSSER BREEDS - what this group includes (read first)

'Bully breeds' refers to a family of breeds (the molossers/mastiff-type dogs), not only pit bulls. It includes: American Pit Bull Terrier, American Staffordshire Terrier, Staffordshire Bull Terrier, American Bully, American Bulldog, English/Olde English Bulldog, French Bulldog, Boston Terrier, Bull Terrier, Boxer, Rottweiler, Cane Corso, Dogo Argentino, Presa Canario, Bullmastiff/Mastiff, Dogue de Bordeaux, Great Dane, and their mixes.

The label describes body type, not temperament - individual dogs vary widely, and behavior is shaped far more by training, handling, and environment than by breed. The rescues below welcome the full range.

This page lists all-'bully' rescues first (they take the whole group), then breed-specific groups within it (Rottweiler, Boxer, Cane Corso, Mastiff/Great Dane, Bulldog, Doberman, Bull Terrier).

NOTE on breed-national rescues: many groups on the ACC New Hope list are single-breed clubs based in NJ/CT/PA/upstate that adopt INTO the NY metro area. They are included here because they serve NYC adopters, even though they aren't NYC-based. For a breed not listed, search '[breed] club of America rescue' - the national parent club will route to the nearest volunteer coordinator.

Breed-club coordinators change often, so confirm the current New York contact before relying on any of these.
