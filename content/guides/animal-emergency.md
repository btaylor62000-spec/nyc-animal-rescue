# Animal emergencies and poison control

_Where to go right now if an animal is injured, collapsed, bleeding, or has swallowed something toxic._

If an animal has been hit by a car, is bleeding, is not breathing, is having a seizure, cannot pass urine, has collapsed or has eaten something toxic, take it to a 24-hour emergency hospital now. Ring on the way so they are ready for you. This page is for medical emergencies; a rescue group cannot help in time.

## FIND THE NEAREST HOSPITAL OPEN NOW

- Every emergency hospital in and near the city, open now and nearest to you first: [Find help](/directory?need=emergency-vet)
- The same hospitals, with their numbers, are listed at the end of this page.
- Hours change. Ring before you set off, even to a hospital listed as open 24 hours.
- If you cannot get the animal there yourself, AmbuVet runs a 24-hour pet ambulance: (800) 262-8838. Ring the hospital you are going to before the pickup.

## IF IT SWALLOWED OR TOUCHED SOMETHING TOXIC: RING POISON CONTROL FIRST

- ASPCA Animal Poison Control (APCC): (888) 426-4435 - 24/7, vet toxicologists.
- Pet Poison Helpline: (855) 764-7661 - 24/7 backup line.
- Lilies are deadly to cats even in tiny amounts (a lick of pollen). Also dangerous: antifreeze, rat and mouse poison, ibuprofen and acetaminophen, xylitol, onion and garlic. Do not make the animal vomit unless a toxicologist tells you to.
- Poison control works out the exact treatment and gives it to the emergency hospital, which saves time when you arrive.

## IN THE BRONX

- No animal hospital in the Bronx is confirmed open 24 hours. Urgent Vets, 3800 E Tremont Ave, (347) 281-9700, is open every day, 10am-10pm.
- Outside those hours, the nearest hospitals open 24 hours are Urgent Vets in Queens, 32-33 Junction Blvd, (718) 507-5600; Animal Specialty Center in Yonkers, 9 Odell Plaza, (914) 457-4000; and the Manhattan hospitals listed at the end of this page.

## IF YOU ARE WORRIED ABOUT PAYING

- Go anyway, and ask the hospital about payment plans when you arrive. Help with emergency bills, from hospital funds to charities and payment plans: [Help paying for veterinary care](/guides/help-paying-for-care)
