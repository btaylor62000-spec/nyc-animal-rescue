# Reporting animal abuse or neglect

_Who to call in New York City, and what to do._

If something worries you, report it. You do not need proof, and you can stay anonymous.

## WHAT TO DO

- 1. If it is happening right now, or an animal or a person is in danger: call 911.
- 2. Anything else: call 311, or report online at portal.311.nyc.gov, and add photos if you have them. Report again every time you see it.
- 3. Then email the NYC Mayor's Office of Animal Welfare (animalwelfare@cityhall.nyc.gov) and your City Council member (find yours by address at https://council.nyc.gov/districts/). Say what you saw, where and when, and give your 311 report numbers. They can bring the city agencies together on it.

## WHAT TO WRITE DOWN

- When and where: date, time, and the address or nearest cross streets.
- What you saw, and how many times you have seen it.
- The animal, and the person if you saw them.
- Photos or video, only if you can take them safely.

## IF NOTHING SEEMS TO HAPPEN

- 311 often closes reports without anyone coming out, and police may arrive late and close a report if they do not see the abuse themselves. Keep reporting every time, with dates and photos: repeated reports are what get action. Send them to the Mayor's Office and your Council member too.

## WHO TO CONTACT

- NYC 311 - 311 - portal.311.nyc.gov - any abuse or neglect that is not happening right now. Goes to your local police precinct. You can add photos and stay anonymous.
- NYC Mayor's Office of Animal Welfare - animalwelfare@cityhall.nyc.gov - https://www.nyc.gov/site/animalwelfare/about/contact-us.page - once you have reported it, to bring city agencies together on it. The office's head has said publicly that requests for help should go to this address. It is a very small office, so do not expect a quick reply.
- Your City Council member - https://council.nyc.gov/districts/ - the member for the district where it happened. Enter the address to find them; their page gives the office's email and phone.
- NYC Council Animal Welfare Caucus - kgomez@council.nyc.gov - https://council.nyc.gov/caucuses/animal-welfare-caucus/ - the Council members who work on animal welfare. The Council publishes this email for questions and concerns.
- NYPD Crime Stoppers - (800) 577-8477 - crimestoppers.nypdonline.org - an anonymous tip, by phone or online. Also reachable as 800-577-TIPS.
- Your local NYPD precinct - nyc.gov/site/nypd/bureaus/patrol/find-your-precinct.page - to report in person. Ask for the ASPCA Liaison Officer, or the Community Affairs Officer.
- HSUS dogfighting tip line - donorcare@humaneworld.org - (202) 452-1100 - https://www.humaneworld.org/en/report-animal-cruelty-contact-form - suspected dogfighting or cockfighting that is not happening right now (if it is, call 911). Email it: Humane World for Animals (formerly the HSUS) passes it to its animal cruelty team, who get in touch with you. Your identity is protected, and it offers a reward of $5,000 for information leading to a conviction.
- ASPCA Community Engagement - when the owner is struggling and needs help with food, vet care or supplies rather than punishment. Refer them by email to referrals@aspca.org.

## WHO TO CONTACT ABOUT A BUSINESS: A PET SHOP, BREEDER, SHELTER OR RESCUE

- Report how a business or a rescue keeps or sells animals here too. If an animal is being hurt, call 311 or 911 first.
- New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets - https://agriculture.ny.gov/animals/animal-related-complaints - two complaint forms. For a pet shop, breeder, shelter or rescue (how it cares for its animals, its premises, or its records): https://agriculture.ny.gov/animals/pet-dealer-and-shelter-rescue-complaint-form . For farm animals such as cattle, goats, sheep or pigs (someone buying, selling or rescuing them without a state permit, a disease the law says must be reported, or a broken quarantine): https://agriculture.ny.gov/form/domestic-animal-health-permit-bu . Not for cruelty: the state says cruelty is for local police.
- Better Business Bureau - https://www.bbb.org/file-a-complaint - a complaint about any business, such as a groomer, boarding kennel, pet shop or breeder. For a seller who turned out to be fake, choose "Report a Scam".

## WHO TO CONTACT ABOUT SOMETHING ONLINE

- Report the post to the website or app too. If you know where in New York City the animal is, call 311, or 911 if it is happening now.
- FBI tips - (212) 384-1000 - https://tips.fbi.gov - videos or posts of animals being deliberately tortured or killed, which is a federal crime. (212) 384-1000 is the FBI's New York office, 24 hours a day.
- FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) - https://www.ic3.gov - online scams, such as paying for a puppy or kitten that never existed. IC3 is for fraud and scams only.

## IF PEOPLE ARE AT RISK TOO

Animal abuse often occurs alongside domestic violence, child abuse and elder abuse. If you are afraid for your own safety or someone else's, or an abuser is using a pet to threaten or control you, use these lines. Many domestic violence shelters can now keep pets safe too, so fear of leaving an animal behind does not have to trap someone. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.

- NYC Domestic Violence Hotline - (800) 621-4673 - nyc.gov/content/endgbv/pages/hotlines - abuse of a person alongside a pet, safety planning, leaving safely. Also reachable as 800-621-HOPE. 24/7, confidential, multilingual. Takes anonymous calls.
- URI PALS - (646) 588-0030 - urinyc.org/uripals - for a survivor who needs to leave but cannot abandon a pet. A New York City shelter programme that houses survivors together with their pets: dogs, cats and small animals.
- Safe Horizon - (212) 577-7700 - https://www.safehorizon.org - works with animal shelters, foster homes and boarding to keep a survivor's pet safe while they leave.
- Volunteers of America-Greater New York - (855) 643-7473 - https://www.voa-gny.org - domestic violence shelters where pets and people stay together.
- Safe Havens for Pets (Animal Welfare Institute) - https://www.safehavensforpets.org - every place that keeps pets safe for people leaving abuse, searchable by zip code.
- Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (988) - 988 - 988lifeline.org - if you or someone else needs mental health or crisis support. Free, confidential, multilingual, 24/7. New York City routes its mental health crisis line through 988. Takes anonymous calls.
- ASPCA Animal Poison Control - (888) 426-4435 - aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control - if an animal has been poisoned or exposed to a toxin. Staffed 24/7, 365 days a year.
