# Exotic pet emergencies

_Rabbits, birds, reptiles and small mammals need an exotic-capable vet. Call ahead._

> Exotic-vet & emergency care

Why a separate list: most general small-animal vets do NOT treat rabbits, birds, reptiles or other exotics. Exotic species hide illness and decline fast, so line up a rabbit/bird/'exotics-savvy' vet BEFORE you need one. Confirm each clinic still sees your species, and their current hours and emergency policy, by phone on the clinic's own line before relying on it.

## Finding an exotics vet you can trust

- Rabbit Rescue & Rehab keeps a rabbit-savvy vet-care page: rabbitrescueandrehab.org/vet-care.
- Bunnies & Beyond keeps a NYC rabbit/guinea-pig vet list: bunniesandbeyond.org/vets.
- NYTTS keeps a turtle/reptile Care & Veterinarians page: nytts.org (Care/Veterinarians).
- The Association of Avian Veterinarians (aav.org) and the Association of Reptilian & Amphibian Veterinarians (arav.org) both have 'find a vet' tools for board-experienced exotic vets.

## 24/7 emergency + specialty (call ahead - confirm they take your species that night):

- The Schwarzman Animal Medical Center (AMC), 510 East 62nd Street, Manhattan, NY 10065 - (212) 838-8100 - 24/7/365 ER with a dedicated Avian & Exotic Pet Medicine Service treating birds, reptiles, guinea pigs, rabbits and other small mammals (amcny.org/pet-owners/specialties/avian-exotic-pet-medicine). No appointment needed for emergency/urgent care; a new ER opened Dec 2025. One of the few NYC ERs with in-house, board-certified exotics specialists and CT/MRI/ultrasound/endoscopy on site. Also accepts injured raptors and waterfowl (per NYC Bird Alliance). AMC's exotic service (est. 1984) was NYC's first; treats birds, rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets, small rodents and reptiles.

## Dedicated exotics day-practice (routine + sick visits, not 24/7):

- The Center for Avian & Exotic Medicine, 562 Columbus Avenue, Manhattan, NY 10024 - (212) 501-8750 - open 7 days 9am-7pm. NYC's only exclusively avian & exotic veterinary hospital (a calm, dog/cat-free environment). Treats birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, small mammals, small ruminants, and wildlife-as-pets (e.g. squirrels, pigeons); offers TeleVet for established patients. The natural complement to AMC's ER for non-emergency exotic care. (On Columbus Ave, near the Wild Bird Fund.)
- Long Island Bird & Exotics Vet Clinic (Nassau County) - the only avian & exotic hospital in Nassau; serves all of NYC (Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, Bronx, Staten Island) plus Westchester/NJ/CT. Treats birds, reptiles, parrots, sugar gliders, rabbits, chinchillas and other small mammals; offers endoscopy, anesthesia, minimally invasive surgery, emergency services, MEDICAL BOARDING and grooming (birdexoticsvet.com). A strong exotics-only option for outer-borough/LI residents.
- Vinegar Hill Veterinary Group, 57 Front St, Unit 101, Brooklyn (DUMBO) 11201 - (718) 797-6875 - Mon-Fri 8am-8pm, Sat-Sun 10am-5pm (2nd location in Brooklyn Heights); vhvetgroup.com. A full-service clinic with the equipment and expertise to treat exotic & avian species (incubators, oxygen therapy, nebulization) alongside cats/dogs; sees guinea pigs, turtles, hedgehogs and birds, and treats some WILDLIFE by appointment (Drs. Neumann & Adelman). The most convenient exotics-capable full-service option in Brooklyn.
- BluePearl and other regional emergency hospitals list exotic capability unevenly - always phone first to confirm an exotics-capable clinician is on that shift.

## Poison control (exotics are highly sensitive - lilies, avocado, teflon fumes, many houseplants can be fatal):

These lines cover exotic pets, not just cats and dogs. Have the species, weight, and the substance/amount ready.

Note on wildlife

- ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center: (888) 426-4435 - 24/7 (a consultation fee may apply). aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control.
- Pet Poison Helpline: (855) 764-7661 - 24/7 (fee applies).
- A wild animal is not a candidate for a pet-clinic visit - route it to a licensed wildlife rehabber or the Wild Bird Fund (646-306-2862). See the Wildlife rehabilitation tab.
