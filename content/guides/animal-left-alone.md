# An animal left alone in a home

_A pet left behind after its owner died, went into hospital or jail, or was evicted: who to call, and how to get your own animal back._

When a pet is left behind in an apartment or house because its owner died, is in hospital or jail, or was evicted. From 311's page on stray and abandoned animals, https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02300 , and the NYC Office of Animal Welfare's page on evictions, https://www.nyc.gov/site/animalwelfare/resources/evictions.page

## If an animal is in danger right now

- If it is happening right now, or an animal or a person is in danger: call 911.

## If a neighbour's pet has been left behind

- 311 takes reports of “a pet that has been abandoned due to an owner's death, hospitalization, or incarceration”. Call 311. Animal Care Centers of NYC (ACC) takes these reports by phone during business hours.
- Have ready: the exact address, what the animal looks like, how it seems, how long it has been alone, and the owner's details if you know them. Take photos only if it is safe.
- If the animal is not being fed or given water, that is neglect. Report it to 311 as animal abuse or neglect: [Reporting animal abuse or neglect](/guides/report-animal-abuse).

## If you were evicted and your animal is inside

- “The marshal or the sheriff will leave a notice on the door indicating to which shelter your animals have been taken if they have been removed and are no longer in the apartment.” In New York State, the marshal or sheriff carrying out an eviction must check for animals and arrange for them to be taken out safely.
- If your animal is still inside: try to coordinate with the building staff or landlord to get your animal first. Keep a record of your correspondence.
- If you still need help getting your animal, call your local police precinct.
- Under NYC law (Administrative Code section 17-819), you may be able to take legal action against a landlord who keeps your animal from you after an eviction.

## If you are facing eviction

- Plan for your animal now: find someone who could look after it, and keep its vaccinations up to date and copies of its vet records with you.
- Call 311 and ask for the Tenant Helpline. Ask whether you qualify for free legal help. More ways to keep you and your pet together: [Keeping your pet if you are losing your home](/guides/keep-your-pet-if-you-lose-your-home).
- Going into hospital? PAWS NY's Hospital Respite Program helps people who are hospitalized or in rehab care for their pets: https://pawsny.org
