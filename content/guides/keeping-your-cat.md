# Help keeping your cat

_Food, vet bills, housing, and temporary care, before surrender becomes the only option._

> OWNER SUPPORT & KEEPING YOUR CAT - help before surrender (contacts inline)

Resources for cat owners who need help - food, in-home care, temporary/crisis fostering, vet-bill aid, housing support, and pet insurance. ACC offers many of these directly and can talk through options. Confirm current availability, which changes over time.

## ACC OWNER SUPPORT / SURRENDER PREVENTION

- ACC CommunityPets - CommunityPets@nycacc.org - (212) 788-4000. Free/reduced vet referrals, spay/neuter, deferred/managed intake, and help during a crisis so a cat can stay in its home. Ask about the surrender-prevention questionnaire; a counselor follows up.
- ACC may also provide supplies (food, litter, carriers) and connect owners to other agencies. To rehome a cat yourself (often less stressful for the cat than shelter intake), ask about ACC's 'Rehoming Your Pet' tools.

## PET FOOD PANTRIES (specific programs)

- ACC Pet Food Pantry - at the ACC Bronx Resource Center. ACC's own pages no longer mention it (checked 2026-11-04), so email to ask before going. CommunityPets@nycacc.org - (212) 788-4000.
- PuppyKitty NYC - runs a pet-food bank; 501(c)(3) rescues can request stock, and it distributes to people in need. foodbank@puppykittynyc.org - puppykittynyc.org.
- Animal Relief Fund (ARF!) - a New York charity that gives pet food to human food pantries. Email susan@arf-ny.org before relying on it: the newest post on its Facebook page we could see is from 2013.
- New York Common Pantry - distributes pet food at events (often with the ASPCA), including in the Bronx. nycommonpantry.org - (917) 720-9700.
- Food Bank For New York City - pet food is sometimes available at member pantries (varies by donations). foodbanknyc.org/get-help.
- ASPCA + Food Bank For NYC partnership - free pet food distributed through Food Bank partner pantries citywide.
- Petco Love 'We Are Family' - collects pet food/litter and distributes through human food-pantry partners across the 5 boroughs; ask your local pantry if they participate.
- Kiso's Pet Food Pantry (Brooklyn) - free pet food, focus on senior citizens. facebook.com/Kisos-Pet-Food-Pantry.
- Feeding Pets of the Homeless - free pet food + vet care via partner sites for people who are homeless or at risk. petsofthehomeless.org.
- Food Help NYC (finder.nyc.gov/foodhelp) lists food pantries, some of which stock pet food; the donateNYC Directory (donatenyc.org) has a Pet Care/Food category.
- NYC's free pet food pantries (new, October 2026) - the city funds free dog and cat food at 22 community food pantries across all five boroughs. The food is new and not every pantry mentions it yet, so ring before you go. All 22, with addresses, nearest first: https://nyc-animal-rescue.pages.dev/directory?need=food-assistance

## SENIOR / DISABILITY / ILLNESS - in-home pet care

- PAWS NY - free in-home help (litter changing, feeding, vet-trip coordination) for low-income seniors and people with illness/disability who struggle with daily pet care. Also runs a pet food pantry. pawsny.org - (212) 203-4760.
- CancerCare Pet Assistance & Wellness (PAW) Program - help with pet-care costs for people in active cancer treatment, with a NYC-resident fund. cancercare.org - (800) 813-4673.

## TEMPORARY / CRISIS CARE - hospitalization, rehab, housing transition

- PAWS NY - Hospital Respite Program for people who are hospitalized or in rehab, plus a Substance Use Recovery Program for those entering inpatient treatment. referrals@pawsny.org - (212) 203-4760 ext. 306.
- Animal Haven Community Engagement - short-term boarding for pets whose owners face a temporary crisis (hospitalization, housing transition); space is limited. (212) 274-8511 - animalhaven.org.
- RescueNYC - holding/recovery space so a caretaker isn't forced to surrender during a TNR or medical situation.
- ACC - can coordinate options for people entering DHS temporary housing. admissions@nycacc.org - (212) 788-4000.

## DOMESTIC-VIOLENCE PET SAFE-KEEPING (free; stay-together or safe foster)

- URI PALS (Urban Resource Institute, People and Animals Living Safely) - the largest DV shelter provider in the U.S.; NYC co-living shelters let survivors bring pets, with private pet-friendly areas. Free. urinyc.org (ask about PALS); NYC DV Hotline (24/7): (800) 621-HOPE / (800) 621-4673.
- Safe Haven Program / MAP Foster - networks of foster homes partnered with DV shelters to care for a pet temporarily when the shelter can't take animals, then reunite. Ask a DV caseworker or the NYC DV Hotline (800) 621-4673.
- PAWS NY - assists with emergency fostering for domestic-violence survivors seeking safety. referrals@pawsny.org - (212) 203-4760 ext. 306.
- RedRover Relief Safe Escape - grants help cover pet boarding/care for survivors leaving abuse. redrover.org/domestic.
- Safe Place for Pets (RedRover) - searchable directory of programs by ZIP. safeplaceforpets.org.
- Safe Havens for Pets (Animal Welfare Institute) - search by zip code for shelters with on-site pet housing, boarding partnerships, or foster care during a crisis: https://awionline.org/safe-havens
- Sheltering Animals & Families Together (SAF-T) - helps DV shelters house pets on-site. https://saftprogram.org.
- SPCA Westchester Pet Safety Program - safe-keeping for pets in DV-affected households (Westchester-adjacent). spcawestchester.org/pet-safety - (914) 941-2896.

## HOSPICE / SENIOR + LIFETIME-CARE placement

- Tavi & Friends (Brooklyn) - senior + FIV lifetime care; the specialist. 646-872-1533 - adopt@taviandfriends.org.
- Cat Castle NYC (Manhattan) - through-senior (up to about 16 years) and hard cases others won't take. (973) 752-7750 - catcastlenyc.org.
- KittyKind (Manhattan) - senior + special-needs placement. adopt@kittykind.org - kittykind.org.
- Lap of Love - in-home hospice and end-of-life veterinary care across NYC. lapoflove.com - (855) 933-5683.
- There is no NYC-only senior-cat sanctuary; the lifetime-care rescues above plus fosters are the route for an unadoptable senior.

## VET BILLS AND PET INSURANCE

- Every way to get help with a vet bill, from charities and hospital funds to payment plans and pet insurance, is in the guide Help paying for veterinary care: https://nyc-animal-rescue.pages.dev/guides/help-paying-for-care
- Or call 311 and ask for current local assistance programmes: in New York City the 211 help-and-referral service is provided through 311.
