# Save an animal from the NYC shelter’s urgent list

_A cat or dog at the city shelter may be euthanised if nobody takes it. Check its status, adopt it yourself or apply through a rescue group, today._

New York City's public animal shelter, Animal Care Centers of NYC (ACC), posts a list of cats and dogs who must leave the shelter quickly. ACC calls it the “at-risk list”. An animal on it may be euthanised if no home or rescue group takes it before the listing closes, so act today.

## STEP 1: FIND THE ANIMAL AND CHECK IT IS STILL AVAILABLE

- Open ACC’s list: https://newhope.shelterbuddy.com/Animal/List
- Tick “Show only available”, then press Filter. An animal marked Reserved already has someone taking it.
- Press “View more..” under the animal. Its page shows its name, its ID number (for example #255268) and which care center it is at. Write these down: you will need them.

## STEP 2: PRESS ONE OF THE TWO BUTTONS ON THE ANIMAL’S PAGE

- “Reserve”: you will adopt the animal yourself. This is the fastest way out. It puts the animal in a cart for 15 minutes. Press “check out” at the top right before the 15 minutes run out and pay the deposit by card; ACC emails you a receipt, and that confirms your reservation. Registering on the site first (“Register”, top of the page) saves time.
- “Pre-Screener Form”: you want to foster or adopt, but through one of ACC’s approved rescue groups instead of adopting from ACC yourself. It opens a Google form, so you need to sign in with a Google account. ACC passes your answers to its rescue groups. Then do Step 3 straight away: do not wait to hear back.
- If the website gives you trouble, email adopt@nycacc.org.

## STEP 3 (IF YOU USED THE PRE-SCREENER FORM): CONTACT RESCUE GROUPS YOURSELF

- Only an approved rescue group can take the animal out for you. ACC calls these groups “New Hope partners”.
- Contact every group in the list below that takes your kind of animal, through every way each one lists (phone, email, form, Instagram), all at once. Do not wait for one reply before trying the next.
- Use the ready-made messages below, so you are not writing one under pressure.
- The animal is safe only when a rescue group tells you it has confirmed the pull with ACC. A friendly reply is not yet a pull. Keep going until you have that confirmation.

## HOW MUCH TIME YOU HAVE

New animals are posted on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 6:00 PM. Each posting closes at 12:00 noon two days later: Sunday’s list closes Tuesday at noon, Tuesday’s closes Thursday at noon, Thursday’s closes Saturday at noon.

ACC says being on the list does not automatically mean an animal will be euthanised: that happens only when no placement has been found. Acting fast is how a placement is found in time.

## READY-MADE MESSAGES

Fill in the brackets. Saying you have a home ready is what gets a yes.

By phone, or as a voicemail: Hi, I'm calling about an at-risk animal at ACC. Name [ ], ID number [ ], at the [borough] care center, listed until [day and time]. [I am / I have] a committed foster or adopter ready the moment the animal is pulled. Can your organisation pull it as a New Hope partner? I'll complete any application right away and arrange transport. Please call or text me back at [your number].

By email, subject line: Urgent at-risk animal, committed home ready - ID number [ ], listed until [day and time]

By email, body: Name [ ], ID number [ ], [borough] care center, listed until [day and time]. [I am / There is] a committed foster or adopter ready the moment the animal is pulled. Can your organisation pull it as a New Hope partner? I'll complete any application immediately and arrange transport. Reach me at [phone] or [email].

Through an online form (kept short, because forms limit the length): At-risk animal, ID number [ ], [borough] care center, listed until [day and time]. [I am / I have] a committed foster or adopter ready now. Can you pull it as a New Hope partner? I will complete your application and arrange transport immediately. Contact: [phone] / [email].

## WHO TO CONTACT

Contact every organisation that takes your animal's species, through every channel it lists, at the same time. This is a working list of rescue groups approved by ACC (ACC calls them New Hope partners) across the New York, New Jersey and Connecticut area, not every ACC partner; ACC's complete official list is linked at the end of this page. Where an organisation has its own entry in this directory, that entry is where its contact details are kept current.
