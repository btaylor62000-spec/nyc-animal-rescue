# Save an animal from the NYC shelter’s urgent list

_A cat or dog at the city shelter may be euthanised if nobody takes it. Check its status, adopt it yourself or apply through a rescue group, today._

New York City's public animal shelter, Animal Care Centers of NYC (ACC), posts an urgent list of cats and dogs who need to leave the shelter quickly. ACC calls it the “at-risk list”. An animal on it may be euthanised if no home or rescue group takes it in time, so act today.

## CHECK THE ANIMAL AND APPLY - START HERE

- See the urgent list, and whether an animal is still available or already reserved: https://newhope.shelterbuddy.com/Animal/List - new animals are posted Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 6 PM.
- To adopt the animal yourself: on that list, press Reserve, then check out (top right) within 15 minutes. It takes a $200 deposit plus a transaction fee: $50 goes toward the adoption fee and $150 is a spay/neuter deposit, refunded if the animal is already spayed or neutered. If the site gives you trouble, email adopt@nycacc.org.
- To foster or adopt through a rescue group instead: fill in ACC's form at https://forms.gle/ozHex3uqvNkQt2dq9 - it needs a Google sign-in. ACC passes your details to its approved rescue groups. Then contact the groups below yourself as well.
- To see what happened to an animal that was listed: https://nycacc.org/adoption/at-risk-outcomes/

## HOW MUCH TIME YOU HAVE

ACC posts the at-risk list three times a week, on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday evenings at 6:00 PM. Each posting stays viewable for 42 hours, until 12:00 noon two days later: Sunday's list closes Tuesday noon, Tuesday's closes Thursday noon, Thursday's closes Saturday noon. Act before the listing closes.

ACC states that being on the at-risk list does not mean an animal is automatically euthanised. Euthanasia happens only when no placement has been secured. The reason to act fast is to secure that placement while the animal is still listed.

## DO THIS NOW, IN ORDER

- Check the animal is still on the list and not reserved, and note its name and A-number: https://newhope.shelterbuddy.com/Animal/List
- If you can give it a home yourself, reserve it on that page now. It is the fastest way out.
- If you cannot adopt directly, or need a rescue group to take it with you as the foster, fill in ACC's form (https://forms.gle/ozHex3uqvNkQt2dq9). If you do not have a Google account to hand, go straight to the next step and come back to it.
- Contact rescue groups yourself, at the same time. Using the list below, contact every group that takes your animal's species, through every channel each one lists, in parallel. Do not wait for one reply before trying the next, and do not assume a phone call is faster than a form or an email. Use the wording below so you are not composing a message under pressure.
- Keep going until a rescue group confirms the pull with ACC. A promising reply is not a pull. The group has to notify ACC and complete their paperwork to take the animal. Only stop once that is confirmed.

## WHAT TO SAY

Fill in the brackets. The committed-home line is the one that gets a yes.

By phone, or as a voicemail: Hi, I'm calling about an at-risk animal at ACC. Name [ ], A-number [ ], at the [borough] centre, listed until [deadline]. [I am / I have] a committed foster or adopter ready the moment the animal is pulled. Can your organisation pull it as a New Hope partner? I'll complete any application right away and arrange transport. Please call or text me back at [your number].

By email, subject line: Urgent at-risk animal, committed home ready - A-number [ ], listed until [deadline]

By email, body: Name [ ], A-number [ ], [borough] centre, listed until [deadline]. [I am / There is] a committed foster or adopter ready the moment the animal is pulled. Can your organisation pull it as a New Hope partner? I'll complete any application immediately and arrange transport. Reach me at [phone] or [email].

Through an online form, kept short because forms cap the length: At-risk animal, A-number [ ], [borough] centre, listed until [deadline]. [I am / I have] a committed foster or adopter ready now. Can you pull it as a New Hope partner? I'll complete your application and arrange transport immediately. Contact: [phone] / [email].
